Care & Handling Notes — please read before ordering
  • Cleaning: Wash with mild antibacterial soap and lukewarm water before and after each use. Never use alcohol or silicone-based cleaners on TPE.
  • Drying & powdering: Pat dry with a soft towel and apply renewal powder (cornstarch) to keep the skin surface silky and prevent tackiness.
  • Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Avoid tight clothing with dark dyes — color can transfer into TPE over time.
  • Makeup wear: Facial makeup on TPE dolls is hand-applied and will gradually fade with handling and cleaning. Makeup wear during normal use is not a quality defect.
  • Positioning: Overseas-warehouse dolls use standard skeletons. Never force joints beyond their natural range — the limit is any pose a normal adult human can hold. Support the body when lifting (approx. 18 kg) and refer to the included manual.
  • Staining & oil: Light surface oil seepage is normal for TPE. Wipe with a dry cloth and re-powder the area.
  • Repairs: Minor nicks can be fixed with the included TPE repair kit. Contact support for anything larger — do not attempt major repairs yourself.
