Returns & Refunds

General Rule: No Reason-Based Returns

Our dolls are personal intimate-health products. For hygiene reasons they cannot be returned or exchanged without a reason ("change of mind" / "no longer wanted"). For confirmed quality issues we offer replacement or compensation only — not returns. By placing an order you accept this policy. Quality issues must be reported within 7 days of receipt; reports after this window cannot be processed.

A. Before Delivery — In-Transit Issues

  • 1. Interception requested while the package is in transit: The customer bears the interception fee plus round-trip shipping (the return leg costs the same as the outbound leg).
  • 2. Unjustified refusal of the parcel: The customer bears round-trip shipping, or ships the parcel back at their own expense — freight-collect (COD) returns are not accepted. A refund is issued only after the warehouse inspects the return and confirms the product is in resalable condition; if the product affects resale, no refund will be issued.
  • 3. Lost package (no tracking movement for an extended period, or an abnormal tracking status): The customer must provide a complete screenshot of the order page, in English (partial screenshots are not accepted), clearly showing the delivery address, tracking number and order value. Once we file the carrier claim, you receive a full refund or a replacement. Cases where the required proof cannot be provided cannot be processed.
  • 4. Address change for a package already in transit: The customer bears the carrier's address-change service fee. Success cannot be guaranteed — if the change fails, the service fee is still charged.
  • 5. Tracking shows "delivered" but you have not received the parcel: If the paid signature service was not purchased, no carrier claim can be filed — we can only provide the Proof of Delivery (POD). To request a carrier investigation you must provide evidence of non-receipt (e.g., doorbell or entrance camera footage). An investigation does not guarantee a successful outcome.

B. After Delivery

  • 1. Damaged packaging or product: If the carton or product is visibly damaged, refuse the parcel at the door. If you have already signed for it, contact us promptly with photos of (1) the outer carton, (2) the carton's shipping label and (3) the damaged product. Compensation is based on the degree of damage; if the product is unusable, we will replace it free of charge or issue a full refund. If a damaged parcel is signed for, returned without reporting first, and warehouse inspection finds the product unusable, no refund will be issued and the customer bears the return shipping cost.
  • 2. Makeup fading is not a quality defect: All dolls stocked in our overseas warehouses are made of TPE; the facial makeup is hand-applied and will gradually wear off with handling and cleaning. Makeup fading during normal use is not a quality issue.
  • 3. Skeleton limits: Overseas-warehouse dolls use standard skeletons. Never force the joints — any pose a normal adult human can physically hold is the limit. Please refer to the included manual.
  • 4. 7-day window: Quality issues must be reported to us within 7 days of receipt. Reports received after this window cannot be processed.
  • 5. No reason-based returns: Our products are personal intimate-health products and do not accept no-reason returns or exchanges. For quality issues we offer replacement or compensation only.
  • 6. Unauthorized returns: If a customer ships a parcel back without our authorization, the warehouse may be unable to locate it, or inspection may find that it affects resale — in either case no compensation or refund will be issued.

How to File a Claim

Email support with your order number, tracking number and clear photos of (1) the shipping carton, (2) the shipping label and (3) the product or defect. Claims are assessed within 3 business days.
